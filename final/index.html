1. Volume Slider with Visual Sound Indicator

Adjust the volume and watch the speaker icon respond to changes.

TRIGGERS
  • Hover On
  • Hover Off
  • Drag Right
  • Drag Left
  • Drag to Zero
  • Release
RULES
  • Hover On:
    • The cursor changes to a hand.
    • The volume slider becomes highlighted.
  • Hover Off:
    • The slider returns to its normal appearance.
  • Drag Right:
    • The volume increases.
    • Additional sound waves appear.
  • Drag Left:
    • The volume decreases.
    • Sound waves gradually disappear.
  • Drag to Zero:
    • All sound waves disappear.
    • An X appears beside the speaker icon.
  • Release:
    • The volume stays at the selected level.
FEEDBACK
  • The slider fill changes with movement.
  • Sound waves appear or disappear according to the volume level.
  • An X appears when the volume reaches zero.
  • The percentage updates as the slider moves.
LOOPS & MODES

No loops or modes.

2. Like Song + Add to Playlist

Use the heart to like a song or the plus icon to add it to another playlist.

TRIGGERS
  • Hover On Heart
  • Hover Off Heart
  • Click Heart
  • Hover On Plus
  • Hover Off Plus
  • Click Plus
  • Click Playlist
RULES
  • Hover On Heart:
    • The cursor changes to a hand.
    • The heart icon becomes highlighted.
  • Hover Off Heart:
    • The heart returns to its normal appearance.
  • Click Heart:
    • The heart fills with green.
    • The song is added to Liked Songs.
  • Hover On Plus:
    • The cursor changes to a hand.
    • The plus icon becomes highlighted.
  • Hover Off Plus:
    • The plus icon returns to its normal appearance.
  • Click Plus:
    • A playlist selection menu appears.
  • Click Playlist:
    • The song is added to the selected playlist.
    • The menu closes.
FEEDBACK
  • The heart fills with green when a song is liked.
  • A confirmation message displays "Added to Liked Songs."
  • The playlist menu appears when the plus icon is clicked.
  • A confirmation message displays "Added to [Playlist Name]."
LOOPS & MODES

No loops or modes.

3. Expandable Queue

Expand the Queue to see more upcoming songs and collapse it to return to the original view.

TRIGGERS
  • Hover On Expand
  • Hover Off Expand
  • Click Expand
  • Hover On Collapse
  • Click Collapse
  • Click Song
RULES
  • Hover On Expand:
    • The cursor changes to a hand.
    • The expand icon becomes highlighted.
  • Hover Off Expand:
    • The icon returns to its normal appearance.
  • Click Expand:
    • The Queue expands into a larger view.
    • The song list becomes wider.
    • More upcoming songs become visible.
  • Hover On Collapse:
    • The cursor changes to a hand.
    • The collapse icon becomes highlighted.
  • Click Collapse:
    • The Queue returns to its compact view.
  • Click Song:
    • The selected song becomes highlighted as playing.
FEEDBACK
  • The Queue expands with a smooth transition.
  • More song titles and artists become visible.
  • The expand icon changes to a collapse icon.
  • The Queue returns to its original size when collapsed.
  • The current playing song remains identifiable.
LOOPS & MODES

No loops or modes.
